## 2026-10-03 - Add the M1 pinned image and confined systemd acceptance

### Result

  five unit regressions onto merged main by content comparison.

  delegation; keep the Workbench 0.2.2 official installer unchanged.

### Why / context

  manifest pins.

  reported.
